Topic introduction

EasyFlow is a unified, AI-first project management workspace for startups and agencies with 3–50 team members.

Tasks and sprint boards

Break projects down and track them to completion.

Documentation

Real-time docs that live next to the work they describe.

Whiteboards

Brainstorm and explain ideas to peers on a shared canvas.

Chat and meeting rooms

Channels for each project, with AI meeting summaries.

AI agent

Work manually, or ask the agent to act, answer, and report.

Access control

Owners choose who can see each project and document.

Integrates with Google Docs One workspace, less clutter Minimal training time

Objectives

Provide small startups and agencies (3–50 members) with one virtual workspace for tasks, documentation, whiteboards, chat, and meeting rooms.

Small teams lack the time and money to buy and learn separate tools, so EasyFlow keeps it simple.

  • Role-based access control, so owners can restrict projects, documents, and channels
  • An AI agent that understands workspace context, acts within the user's permissions, and generates analytics
  • AI meeting summaries, with easy retrieval of past meeting notes
  • Google Docs integration, with WhatsApp where feasible
  • A simple interface a new team can adopt with minimal training

Scope

In scope

  • Project tasks and sprint boards
  • Documentation and whiteboards

  • Access control for projects, documents, and channels

Out of scope

  • Financial managementPayroll, agency profit and loss, financial tracking

Proposed methodology

Scrum: iterative sprints, each delivering a working increment of EasyFlow.

Product backlog

List and prioritise all features.

Sprint planning

Pick the items for the next sprint.

Sprint

Design, build, and test the selected features.

Sprint review

Demo the increment and collect feedback.

Retrospective

Improve the process for the next sprint.

Architecture: MVC backend, component-based frontend, and a database designed for fast, scalable retrieval.
